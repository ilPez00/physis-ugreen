## Owner steps (cannot be done by the agent)

1. Register on https://ugreenhackathon.devpost.com/ ("Join Hackathon") and
   confirm eligibility (Italy is on the eligible Europe list; individuals or
   teams of up to three).
2. Open the submission form and read the ten questions verbatim. The proposal
   already answers the ten official questions confirmed on the Devpost page;
   adjust wording only if the live form differs.
3. Check the track selection (all prizes are open to every track).
4. Upload `physis-casefiles-proposal.pdf` and `physis-casefiles-round1.zip`.
5. Complete team consent / member fields if applicable (excluded from the
   500-word limit).
6. Save the draft, review, then submit before the deadline.
