# Evidence rules

These rules bind every stage of the casefile workflow. They exist because a
reconstruction is only as honest as its weakest claim.

## The three evidence states

- **Supported** — at least one named source directly supports the exact
  assertion. Cite the file and the locator actually returned.
- **Conflicting** — two specific sources make incompatible claims about the
  same thing. Cite and quote every side. Present the disagreement; do not
  resolve it by preference, and do not invent a cause.
- **Not established** — records are missing, inaccessible, ambiguous,
  incomplete or insufficient. Report as an open question, never as a
  conclusion.

Nothing else is a state. A plausible story is not supported; a suspicious
number is not conflicting; a repeated assertion across copies is not
independent corroboration.

## Dating discipline

- A document bearing the date May 7 is evidence that the document bears that
  date. Whether the work it describes happened on May 7 is a separate claim
  needing its own support.
- Distinguish document date, stated event date and inferred order. Use the
  date as written in the source and say which kind it is.
- A photo's capture date is when the photo was taken, not when the events it
  shows happened. OCR output is machine-read text from an image and carries
  the recognition uncertainty actually returned.

## Numbers

- Every amount comes from a quoted span of a named source.
- Compare amounts only when totals, currency, scope, approval status and tax
  treatment are clear; show operands and calculation. Otherwise state why
  comparison is unresolved.
- A charge without a matching approval is reported as a discrepancy. The
  report does not speculate about intent, does not use words like fraud, and
  does not name a responsible party.

## Absence and copies

- The absence of a matching record is not proof the record never existed.
  Missing evidence is listed with what was searched and what was not found.
- Copies of one record are copies, not independent corroboration.
- If the selected folder may not contain everything, the casefile says the
  reconstruction is bounded by the folder it was given.

## Citations

- Every claim carries its source path and the locator actually returned, or
  an explicit statement that no locator was available.
- Quotes are verbatim. Paraphrase is allowed only in surrounding prose, never
  inside quotation marks.
- A source reference without evidence of the asserted claim is not a citation.
